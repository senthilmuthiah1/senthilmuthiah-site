For years I've written about medical billing, EMR and EDI on [emrpms.blogspot.com](https://emrpms.blogspot.com/). Those articles stay right where they are, and I'll keep linking to them.

New posts will live here, next to the rest of my work.

## What I'll write about

- **EMR** — clinical records, charting and documentation
- **PMS** — scheduling, registration and front-office workflow
- **LIS** — lab orders, results and reporting
- **Billing and RCM** — claims, payments, eligibility and EDI
- **Java** — the code behind all of it
