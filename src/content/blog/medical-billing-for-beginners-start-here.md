*Welcome! This is a free, beginner-friendly course in US medical billing. No healthcare background needed.*

My older posts on medical billing, written since 2010, were never in a clear order for someone starting from zero. So I've rewritten the core of them — and added much more — as a **series of short lessons you can read in order**.

Each lesson:

- explains every new word in **plain English**
- follows the same made-up patients — **Anna**, **John** and **Raj** — so ideas build on each other
- has a **diagram**, a short **summary**, a **glossary** and **FAQs**

Start at lesson 1, and go at your own pace.
